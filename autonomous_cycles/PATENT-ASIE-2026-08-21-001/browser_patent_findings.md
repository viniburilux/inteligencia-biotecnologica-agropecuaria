## UEL institutional repository: LABIM22

**Source:** https://repositorio.uel.br/handle/123456789/18265  
**Item:** `Antagonismo da cepa LABIM22 de Bacillus velezensis frente a fungos fitopatógenos e biocontrole do mofo branco em sementes de soja`  
**Author:** Higashi, AllanYukio  
**Date shown:** 2020-02-19  
**Collection:** CCA — Centro de Ciências Agrárias; Mestrado em Agronomia.

The repository abstract describes an in silico genome analysis, in vitro antagonism tests and in vivo evaluation of white-mold control in soybean seeds. It names predicted antifungal metabolites (fengicin, bacillibactin and surfactin), tests against multiple phytopathogenic fungi, stability tests across temperature/light/pH, and a formulated medium identified as a **patent-in-process request**. The abstract also reports seed germination/health outcomes for a LABIM22 treatment and explicitly states that the plant-growth-promotion potential still requires further studies.

This source materially strengthens the LABIM22 branch as a public **research-to-application corridor**, but it remains a repository abstract/metadata observation in this cycle. The attached PDF was not downloaded; no new scientific result is being independently asserted from the abstract.
